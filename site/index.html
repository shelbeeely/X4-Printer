X4 Print Inbox

Turn an Xteink X4 e-paper device into a wireless print inbox and offline approval terminal. A Raspberry Pi Zero W acts as a normal network printer that any OS's built-in print dialog can find — no drivers, no app. The X4 downloads new documents on wake, lets you read and approve or reject them fully offline, and syncs the approval back once it has a connection again.

How a print reaches you

  1. 1 Print from any computer's normal print dialog
  2. 2 The Raspberry Pi receives the job
  3. 3 It retains the original and generates an XTC page for the X4
  4. 4 The X4 wakes and downloads pending documents to its SD card
  5. 5 The X4 goes back offline
  6. 6 You read the document and approve or reject it, fully offline
  7. 7 The approval is saved locally on the X4
  8. 8 When the X4 next has internet access, the approval syncs to the home Pi — directly, or through the relay
  9. 9 The Pi sends the original document to a physical network printer

Design highlights

Looks like a normal printer

Speaks IPP/mDNS well enough for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS's built-in driverless print paths — no app, no driver install.

Never loses a job or an approval

Every durable write is atomic, and approvals are idempotent end-to-end, so retries and reboots can never cause a duplicate physical print.

Respects the memory budget

Downloads and pages stream straight to and from SD — no full-document buffers anywhere in the ESP32-C3 firmware.

Deep sleep by default

The X4 is never remotely wakeable. It wakes from a button press or its own timer, syncs, and goes back to sleep.
